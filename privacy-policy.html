Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2 September 2026

Teen Patti Arena is an entertainment card game using virtual chips only. Virtual chips have no cash value and cannot be withdrawn for money.

1. Information we process

Depending on how you sign in and use the game, we may process:

  • Account identifiers such as a Supabase user ID.
  • Player name or username.
  • Email address when provided by you or by a supported sign-in provider.
  • For Facebook Login, basic profile information made available by Facebook, such as your name, Facebook user identifier, profile picture and email when available and permitted.
  • Game information such as virtual-chip balance, game participation, wins, progress, achievements, challenges, club/social activity and moderation reports.
  • Technical and security information needed to operate the service, prevent abuse and troubleshoot errors.

2. How we use information

We use information to authenticate players, create and maintain player profiles, operate multiplayer rooms, maintain virtual-chip balances and progress, provide social/game features, prevent abuse, secure the service, respond to support requests, and improve reliability.

3. Facebook Login

If you choose Facebook Login, Facebook authenticates your account and may provide permitted profile information to Teen Patti Arena. We do not ask for or store your Facebook password.

4. Guest Login

Guest Login creates an anonymous authentication account for gameplay. Guest progress may become inaccessible if the user signs out, clears application/browser data, or changes device before linking or upgrading the account.

5. Service providers

We use service providers such as Supabase for authentication, database and backend functions. These providers process information only as needed to provide the game infrastructure and are subject to their own security and privacy obligations.

6. Data sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share information with infrastructure/service providers when necessary to operate the game, comply with law, protect users, investigate abuse, or enforce our policies.

7. Data retention

We retain account and game information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain security and integrity, resolve disputes, comply with legal obligations, or until a valid deletion request is completed.

8. Your choices and data deletion

You may request deletion of your Teen Patti Arena account and associated personal data at any time. See our Data Deletion Instructions.

9. Children

Teen Patti Arena is not intended for children below the minimum age required by applicable law for the relevant online service. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

10. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect account and game information. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the game, legal requirements or our data practices change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are published.
