User Data Deletion Instructions

Teen Patti Arena • Nectar™

You can request deletion of your Teen Patti Arena account and associated personal data at any time.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to tejasvinernational2025@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject: Teen Patti Arena Data Deletion Request.
  3. Include the player name used in Teen Patti Arena and the email address used to sign in, if applicable.
  4. If you used Facebook Login, mention that the account was created with Facebook Login. Do not send your Facebook password.

What we delete

After verifying the request, we will delete or anonymize personal account information and associated game profile information that is no longer required, including the player profile and stored identifiers, subject to legal, fraud-prevention, security or dispute-resolution retention requirements.

Facebook removal

You can also remove Teen Patti Arena from your Facebook account through Facebook's Apps and Websites settings. Removing Facebook access stops future Facebook authentication, but to request deletion of data stored by Teen Patti Arena, please also follow the deletion request steps above.

Processing time

Valid deletion requests are processed without undue delay and within the time required by applicable law. We may contact you only when necessary to verify the account associated with the request.
